The offering

A milestone worth marking.

The 2% Club is a personal giving-back initiative by Joshua Cole, owner and tattoo artist at Work of Art Tattoo & Piercing. It celebrates people in recovery reaching substantial sobriety anniversaries—one year, multiple years, and other meaningful milestones.

Selected recipients receive a small, simple 2% tattoo at no charge. The emblem represents resilience and belonging within this initiative; it is not a claim that only 2% of people recover.

01 / A GIFT

Completely free

There is no tattoo charge. Tips are appreciated but entirely optional, and never affect eligibility or quality of care.

02 / REALISTIC CAPACITY

3–4 each month

Joshua hopes to donate three or four appointments monthly, depending on his regular schedule, supplies, and available resources. Availability is never guaranteed.

03 / YOUR STORY IS YOURS

Private by default

No public recovery announcement, testimonial, or photograph is required. A referral starts a private conversation, not an automatic booking.

Why I do this · Step Twelve

Someone helped me.
Now it's my turn.

My name is Joshua Cole. I'm an artist, a shop owner, and a person in recovery. I know what it means to need another chance, and I know what a difference it makes when somebody believes your life is worth investing in.

Recovery gave me the opportunity to rebuild my life. I have a business I love, people I care about, and a future I want to show up for. None of that is something I take for granted.

Step Twelve calls us to carry the message and practice these principles in all our affairs. For me, service isn't just something to talk about at a meeting. It's something I can do with my own hands, with the craft I've spent years learning.

I can't give someone their recovery. But I can celebrate the recovery they've earned.

These tattoos are my way of saying: I see the work you've done. Your life matters. Keep going.

— Joshua Cole, Work of Art Tattoo & Piercing

Referral-only initiative

How to nominate someone.

Recovery sponsors, counselors, program staff, and trusted peers may privately refer someone celebrating a significant sobriety anniversary. Please include the milestone, a brief introduction, and a way to arrange a private conversation with the person's consent.

This program is limited and scheduled case by case. Tattoo size, design, placement, health and safety considerations, and appointment availability are discussed before acceptance. No one is asked to disclose their story publicly or to participate in marketing.

Two initiatives · One purpose

Art in service of a better future.

Second Chance Ink helps people moving away from gang life cover reminders of the past. The 2% Club honors milestones in recovery. Both come from the same conviction: when we have something good to give, we can use it to lift someone else up.
