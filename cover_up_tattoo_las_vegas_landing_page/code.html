Authority FAQ

Nearly everything can be covered or restored with the right approach. Our artists specialize in analyzing existing ink to determine the most effective saturations and designs for a seamless transformation.

In 90% of cases, laser is not required. Our "Deep-Saturation" technique is designed to mask most pigments. We only recommend laser if the existing ink is exceptionally raised or excessively dark across a massive area.

Cover-ups are quoted based on complexity rather than just size. Because they require advanced custom mapping and higher ink saturation, expect a premium of 20-30% over standard work. Text us a photo for a custom quote.
