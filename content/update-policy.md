# Content Update Policy

Do not update dates for freshness theater.

A page's substantive review date may change only when a real human review or meaningful content update occurs, such as:

- verified business information changes
- artist roster or specialties change
- medical/safety source review changes guidance
- original photography, healed evidence, or case details are added
- pricing, booking, location, or process details change
- substantial structure or factual copy is revised

Maintain `datePublished` and `dateModified` only when known. If a page only receives formatting, typo cleanup, or internal tooling changes, do not claim a new substantive review date.
