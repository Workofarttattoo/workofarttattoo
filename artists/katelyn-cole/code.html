The Artist & The Authority

About Katelyn Cole

Katelyn is Work of Art's professional piercer, with a placement-first approach built around anatomy, jewelry fit, and a calm client experience.

For ear curation, she looks at the ear as a whole before suggesting individual placements. She marks placement with the client involved, explains when anatomy changes the best option, and plans initial jewelry with swelling and long-term wear in mind.

What an appointment is like

Appointments are practical and unhurried: anatomy check, placement mark, jewelry discussion, piercing, and clear aftercare. Single-use needles and the studio's sterilization and surface-disinfection procedures are part of the normal setup.

Aftercare and follow-up

Katelyn talks through pressure, snagging, sleep, headphones, swimming, downsizing, and other everyday factors that can affect healing. Healing time varies by placement, anatomy, jewelry, aftercare, pressure, and individual biology.

Families welcome

Piercing Minors — Rules & What to Bring

Katelyn Cole specializes in calm ear piercing for younger clients. Sessions are unhurried, explained step-by-step, and designed so kids and guardians feel confident — most families tell us how smoothly everything goes.

Who we can pierce

  • Ages 14 and up for ear piercing with a parent or legal guardian present the entire appointment.
  • The accompanying adult must be the legal guardian — we work with you when you are the guardian on record, not only a family friend or relative unless they are the documented guardian.
  • Ear piercing focus for minors; other placements are evaluated case-by-case at consultation.

What to bring

  • Minor's government-issued ID (or school ID with photo where applicable).
  • Guardian's government-issued photo ID.
  • Minor's birth certificate linking the guardian to the client.
  • Both minor and guardian must be present for the full service — no drop-offs.

Questions before you drive over? Call ahead at (725) 224-1240 or Email us! — we will clarify paperwork, placement, and jewelry so your appointment is seamless.

Questions clients ask

How do I prepare for an Ear Curation consultation?

Simply book a "Curation Consultation" with Katelyn. Bring photos of ear styles you like, but keep an open mind—Katelyn will map your specific anatomy to see what placements will heal best and look most harmonious for you.

What jewelry materials does Katelyn use?

We use properly fitted initial jewelry selected for the placement and your anatomy.

Who should I book for a tattoo at Work of Art?

Katelyn is a professional piercer. For tattoos — including fine line, realism, and large-scale work — book with Joshua Cole or Teralyn through our appointments page.

What is the healing time for a cartilage piercing?

Cartilage piercings generally take 6 to 12 months to fully mature. Katelyn provides aftercare guidance designed to support normal healing and help you recognize when irritation needs attention.

Can I bring my own jewelry for a new piercing?

For new piercings, we use jewelry the studio can verify for material, fit, and sterilization. We can, however, help you transition to your own high-quality heirloom pieces once the piercing is fully healed.

Who does ear curation at Work of Art?

Katelyn Cole leads ear curation and piercing planning at Work of Art in Las Vegas, using each client's anatomy and style goals to shape a balanced layout.

Are there professional female piercers near the Strip?

Yes. Katelyn is Work of Art's professional piercer at the Tropicana studio east of the Strip. She offers ear, facial, and body piercing with anatomy-aware placement and direct booking.

How does Work of Art choose starter jewelry?

At Work of Art Tattoo & Piercing, Katelyn Cole plans starter jewelry around anatomy, placement, swelling room, and current studio availability.
