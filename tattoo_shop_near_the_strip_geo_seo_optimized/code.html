Getting here

Strip rideshare, taxi, and parking notes

Use 2375 E. Tropicana Ave, Suite 3 as the destination. From most Strip resorts, rideshare is easier than driving through casino garages, but taxi lines can be faster when Lyft or Uber surge after shows.

Lyft/Uber planning

  • South Strip/MGM area: usually about $10-22 before surge.
  • Center/north Strip: often about $15-30 before surge.
  • After arena, Sphere, festival, or nightclub traffic, compare taxi and rideshare before requesting.

Taxi planning

  • Most major hotels have taxi queues at valet/transportation.
  • Official LAS taxi companies include A Cab, Deluxe, Desert, Lucky, Nellis, Western, Whittlesea/Henderson, and Yellow Checker Star.
  • Ask for the direct Tropicana route to the studio.

Tattoo timing

  • Book before pool time, heavy sun, or drinking.
  • Keep fresh tattoos out of hotel pools and hot tubs.
  • Use our free lot if you drive; no casino parking validation is needed.

Ride-share prices are planning ranges, not quotes. Lyft says estimates can change by route, ride type, availability, demand, stops, destination changes, delayed requests, discounts, and traffic. Airport pickup and taxi details are based on Harry Reid ride-share guidance and airport taxi company information reviewed August 21, 2026.
