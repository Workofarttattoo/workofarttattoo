# Source Policy

Use firsthand Work of Art evidence for firsthand claims:

- original portfolio images
- healed/fresh comparison photos
- artist observations
- studio process notes
- actual client cases with consent

Use authoritative external evidence for health, medical, wound-healing, infection, allergy, and material-safety claims. Preferred sources include government health agencies, recognized medical institutions, peer-reviewed literature, and professional piercing standards.

Do not rewrite competitor blogs as evidence. Do not let citations replace the studio's own proof. Do not diagnose medical conditions. Use language such as "Contact a qualified medical professional if..." where appropriate.
