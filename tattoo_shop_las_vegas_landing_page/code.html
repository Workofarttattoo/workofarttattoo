Do you accept walk-ins? expand_more
Yes, we welcome walk-ins every day! However, availability depends on our artists' schedules. For guaranteed same-day service, we recommend texting us ahead of time.
How should I prepare for my session? expand_more
Stay hydrated, eat a full meal beforehand, and avoid alcohol 24 hours prior. Wear comfortable clothing that allows easy access to the area being tattooed.
