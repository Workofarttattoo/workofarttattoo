Knowledge Base

Fine Line Essentials

What is fine line? expand_more
Fine line tattooing uses small needle groupings (often just one needle) to create delicate, thin, and intricate designs. It's an art form focused on subtlety and elegance rather than bold, thick borders.
Do they fade faster? expand_more
With proper application by a specialist, they do not "fade" faster, but like all tattoos, they soften over time. We use specific techniques and inks designed to maintain integrity for decades, not just weeks.
Can you do single needle? expand_more
Yes, we specialize in single-needle work. It requires extreme precision and steady hands—it's what we are known for in the Las Vegas community.
Walk-in availability? expand_more
While we prioritize appointments for custom fine line work, we occasionally have same-day openings. Your best bet is to text us at 725-224-1240 for immediate availability.
