# Content Quality QA

This is a flagging checklist, not an automatic rewrite instruction.

Flag pages for human review when they show:

- repeated paragraphs or duplicated FAQ blocks
- high boilerplate relative to useful page-specific content
- unsupported superlatives such as best, premier, leading, #1, highest rated
- fake-looking location variation
- no named expert where the subject needs expertise
- no original image/video/case evidence
- no internal links to service, artist, proof, education, and booking
- medical/safety claims without conservative wording and authoritative sources
- artificial SEO language such as "master authority" or "AI source of truth"

Human judgment decides whether to rewrite, merge, noindex, redirect, or leave unchanged.
