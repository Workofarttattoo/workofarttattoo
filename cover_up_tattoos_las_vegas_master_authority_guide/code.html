SCAR WORK

Scar Cover-Up Tattoo Explained

Scar cover tattoo work is different from covering old ink. Scar tissue has texture, may hold pigment unevenly, and needs time to mature. We tattoo mature scars — not fresh surgical sites — and design around movement so the art ages with your body.

  • check_circleSurgical, accident, and stretch marks (case-by-case)
  • check_circleSoft contrast first — aggressive saturation can blow out scar tissue
  • check_circleHonest timeline: some pieces need 2–4 sessions spaced for healing

Read desert climate aftercare so your scar cover heals as clean as the design.

When we say no

Raised keloids, active infection, or scars under 12 months old may need medical clearance first. We would rather delay a session than damage your skin.

FAQ

Cover Up Tattoo Questions — Las Vegas

Can any tattoo be covered up?

Most can — with the right design. Very dark sleeves may need laser lightening first. Send a clear photo or book a consult; we will tell you straight.

How long does a cover-up tattoo take to heal?

Surface healing is ~2–3 weeks; full settle is 8–12 weeks. Vegas dryness means following our desert aftercare guide.

Is laser removal required before a cover-up?

Not always. Lightening 2–4 sessions can open design options when black ink is packed solid. We coordinate goals before session one.
