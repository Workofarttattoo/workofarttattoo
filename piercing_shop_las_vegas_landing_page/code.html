Common Questions

Do you use needles or guns? expand_more

Absolutely never. We only use single-use, medical-grade piercing needles. Piercing guns cause blunt force trauma to tissue and cannot be properly sterilized in an autoclave. Our needle technique is cleaner, more precise, and leads to much faster healing.

Do you have gold jewelry in stock? expand_more

Yes, we maintain one of the largest inventories of solid 14k and 18k gold jewelry in Las Vegas. We carry world-renowned brands like BVLA and Anatometal, featuring genuine diamonds, opals, and precious gemstones.

How should I prep for my appointment? expand_more

Eat a light meal 1-2 hours before coming in, stay hydrated, and bring a valid government-issued ID. Wear comfortable clothing that allows easy access to the piercing area. If you're doing an ear curation, bringing photos of styles you like is helpful!

Do you take walk-ins? expand_more

Yes, we welcome walk-ins every day! However, for complex ear curations or during weekend nights, we highly recommend calling ahead or texting us to check current wait times or to secure a specific time slot.
