Tattoo Shop Near Las Vegas Airport (LAS)

Landing at Harry Reid International (LAS)? Work of Art is a straight shot east on Tropicana — closer than fighting Strip traffic for a serious consult.

Flying out within 48 hours of a fresh tattoo? Ask us — cabin pressure and dry airplane air change aftercare timing.

Directions from your area

  • From LAS Terminal 1 or 3: rideshare or rental ~8–12 minutes east on Tropicana to 2375 E. Tropicana Ave, Suite 3.
  • Do not detour through the Strip for navigation — Tropicana is the direct route from the airport.
  • Returning rental: most agencies are airport-side; plan tattoo sessions before drop-off if you fly same day.

Airport pickup, Lyft, Uber, and taxis

From Harry Reid International Airport, Work of Art is close enough that a direct ride usually makes more sense than going to the Strip first. Airport ride-share pickup is not at the curb: Terminal 1 pickup is on Level 2 of the parking garage; Terminal 3 pickup is on the Valet Level of the parking garage.

Ride-share planning

  • Typical non-surge Lyft/Uber planning range from LAS to the studio: about $12-25.
  • Terminal walks can add 5-10 minutes before your car arrives.
  • Roadwork or tunnel delays near LAS can add time through December 2026.

Taxi planning

  • Taxis are available outside baggage claim at both terminals.
  • Airport/Strip flat rates apply to Strip zone trips; the studio is usually a short metered ride.
  • Ask the cab stand for the most direct Tropicana route to 2375 E. Tropicana Ave.

Ride-share prices are planning ranges, not quotes. Lyft says estimates can change by route, ride type, availability, demand, stops, destination changes, delayed requests, discounts, and traffic. Airport pickup and taxi details are based on Harry Reid ride-share guidance and airport taxi company information reviewed August 21, 2026.

Plan the day before or after a flight

Airport visitors should keep the day simple: land, hydrate, eat, come to the studio, then choose low-friction landmarks instead of pool-heavy plans. The Welcome to Fabulous Las Vegas sign, UNLV/Thomas & Mack area, and south Strip are practical nearby options.

  • Do not schedule a large tattoo right before a long flight without asking about aftercare timing.
  • Leave room for airport rideshare pickup walks and possible connector/tunnel delays.
  • If you are flying out the same night, choose a consult, small tattoo, piercing check, or pre-planned quick session.

Show schedules, prices, and dark dates change. Check the venue schedule before building your appointment day; for fresh tattoos and piercings, avoid pool time, heavy sun, and heavy drinking after the appointment.

Things to do afterward

Afterward, keep the plan close and flexible: Welcome to Fabulous Las Vegas sign, UNLV/Thomas & Mack, Town Square, or AREA15 if you have several hours before a flight. Check bags, ride-share timing, and aftercare supplies before heading to the airport.

AREA15 is at 3215 S Rancho Dr with immersive attractions, food/drink, events, free parking, and daily district hours listed as 11:30 AM-10 PM; tenant experience hours vary. Always check hours, ticketing, age policies, and event schedules before you go.
