How is a premium tattoo studio different from a Strip walk-in shop?

Premium studios prioritize consults, sterile setup, artist continuity, and healed portfolio proof. Walk-in booths optimize speed and tourist volume. Compare hygiene protocols and revision policies — not just price or proximity to the Strip.
