# Review Growth Playbook

Goal: steady, legitimate review velocity without incentives or review gating.

## In-Studio Workflow

- Add a QR/NFC review card at checkout.
- Ask after a successful service while the client is still happy and engaged.
- Use one simple line: "If you loved the experience, a Google review helps local clients find the right artist here."
- Do not offer discounts, gifts, or special treatment for reviews.

## Follow-Up Message

Subject/text: Thank you from Work of Art

Message: Thank you for coming in today. If you are happy with your tattoo or piercing experience, a Google review helps other Las Vegas clients choose the right studio and artist. Here is the review link: [insert official GBP review link]

## Staff Workflow

- Confirm client is satisfied.
- Provide aftercare instructions first.
- Ask for review second.
- Record no private health details in any review workflow.
- Respond to new reviews weekly.
