Questions clients ask

Where can I get a fine line tattoo in Las Vegas? +

Work of Art Tattoo & Piercing at 2375 E. Tropicana Ave, Suite 3 — minutes from the Strip. Joshua Cole specializes in fine line tattoos in vegas. Book at workofarttattoo.com/appointments/ or call (725) 224-1240.

How much does a fine line tattoo cost in Las Vegas? +

Fine Line Tattoos in Vegas pricing depends on size, placement, and session count. Work of Art quotes after a consult — transparent pricing before you sit in the chair. See our tattoo pricing guide for ranges.

How do I book a fine line tattoo appointment? +

Book online at workofarttattoo.com/appointments/ or call (725) 224-1240. Custom fine line tattoo work starts with a consult and healed-photo goals, not a walk-in flash sheet.

Is Work of Art near the Las Vegas Strip? +

Yes — 2375 E. Tropicana Ave, Suite 3, about 10 minutes from major Strip resorts. Private parking, licensed studio, desert-climate aftercare coaching included.

Who should I see for fine line tattoo at Work of Art? +

For fine line, fineline floral work, script, custom drawings by commission, and high-detail smaller tattoos, see Teralyn at /artists/teralyn/. For larger custom realism, blackwork, sleeves, and cover-ups, see Joshua Cole at /artists/joshua-cole/.
