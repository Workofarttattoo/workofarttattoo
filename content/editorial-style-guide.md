# Editorial Style Guide

Work of Art voice should be expert, plain-English, artistic, firsthand, concise, and technically precise.

Prefer:

- direct answers before long explanations
- named artists only where expertise is real
- specific placement/style/process details
- original images and healed evidence
- conservative health wording
- useful local Las Vegas context when it actually matters

Avoid:

- keyword stuffing
- fake medical authority
- fake awards or credentials
- "In today's ever-changing world"
- "When it comes to"
- "Whether you're"
- "Nestled in the heart of"
- "Look no further"
- unsupported "best," "premier," "leading," "#1," or "highest rated" claims
- pages that exist only for keyword variations
