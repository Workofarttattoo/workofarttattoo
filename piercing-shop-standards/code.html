Helix piercing in Las Vegas

Helix body piercing is one of our most requested cartilage services — upper-helix, forward helix, and stacked helix points planned as one composition. Unlike mall piercing guns, every helix piercing here uses sterile needles, starter jewelry selected for the placement, and anatomy mapping so the ring or stud sits flush and heals in desert climate.

We stock helix-friendly hoops and labrets; premium helix piercing jewelry can be ordered ahead for titanium builds. Pair helix work with lobe or conch points for a full curated ear — see healed helix examples in our gallery below.
