Teralyn

Fineline floral, script, and detailed small tattoo artist at Work of Art Tattoo & Piercing in Las Vegas.

Teralyn works in both piercing and tattooing. She came through the studio's piercing apprenticeship and also tattoos clean, approachable pieces clients can book without overcomplicating the idea.

Her tattoo lane is fineline floral work, fine line, script, tiny symbols, detailed small images, walk-in-friendly pieces, and custom drawings by commission when you want the idea designed for you. She also takes piercing appointments as part of the Work of Art piercing team.
