Before your visit

Studio Rules

These rules help keep tattoo and piercing appointments calm, clean, respectful, and legally compliant.

Identification and Age Requirements

Bring valid government-issued identification. Minors require the correct parent or guardian documentation, and not every service is available for minors.

Guests and Studio Conduct

Keep guests limited, respectful, and clear of procedure areas. Arrive sober, fed, hydrated, and ready to discuss placement, design, jewelry, or healing questions honestly.

Walk-Ins and Booking

Walk-ins are welcome when the schedule allows. Booking ahead is the best way to reserve time for custom tattoos, cover-ups, ear curation, facial piercing, body piercing, or jewelry changes.
