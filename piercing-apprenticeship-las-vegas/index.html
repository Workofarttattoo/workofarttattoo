What you will learn

The following curriculum outline covers core professional competencies and sanitation preparation; the instructor will confirm the detailed syllabus and training schedule at enrollment.

  1. Month 1 — Foundations and licensing: bloodborne pathogens, OSHA-compliant training, infection prevention, hand hygiene, PPE, sharps disposal, exposure response, sanitation exam preparation, consent, age and ID requirements, documentation and apprentice card application.
  2. Month 2 — Anatomy and asepsis: anatomy and placement suitability, contraindications, sterile field setup, cleaning and disinfection, sterilization, autoclave monitoring, instrument handling and jewelry materials, sizing and threading.
  3. Month 3 — Core piercing skills: consultation, marking, placement planning, supervised ear and facial piercing, jewelry insertion, client communication and pain/anxiety management.
  4. Month 4 — Expanded practice: anatomy-specific ear curation, body piercing, jewelry selection, symmetry, troubleshooting, and supervised competency development.
  5. Month 5 — Healing and professional practice: aftercare, downsizing, follow-up, recognizing complications and referral, records, photography with consent, ethics, client service and studio operations.
  6. Month 6 — Career readiness: supervised case review, skills assessment, portfolio development, professional boundaries, business basics, experience documentation and permanent Body Art Card application support.

Your path to a government-issued Body Art Card

  1. Be at least 18, provide valid ID, complete qualifying bloodborne pathogen training and register with an eligible SNHD Body Art Mentor at a permitted establishment.
  2. Complete the SNHD apprentice application, pass the sanitation exam with at least 80%, and obtain your Body Art Apprentice Card before apprenticeship activities begin.
  3. Complete at least six months of documented supervised training under an authorized mentor.
  4. When your mentor confirms completion, submit the signed SNHD experience verification form to exchange your apprentice card for a permanent Body Art Card, subject to SNHD requirements and approval.

Our commitment: We provide structured training, licensing preparation and completion documentation for apprentices who meet program and regulatory requirements. The Health District issues the official card; issuance cannot be guaranteed by a private studio. A Clark County Body Art Card is not a nationwide license. Documented experience may support applications elsewhere, but each jurisdiction determines its own requirements.
