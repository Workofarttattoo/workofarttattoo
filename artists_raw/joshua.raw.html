20 Years in Las Vegas

Joshua Cole in Las Vegas

Joshua Cole has spent over 20 years building a black-and-grey realism practice in Las Vegas — one appointment, one healed tattoo, at a time.

His approach started before he ever picked up a machine. Years working in fine dining taught him how to make a stranger feel comfortable in an unfamiliar room. Years in art school, oil painting, and illustration taught him how light and shadow actually behave on a form that isn't paper. Both of those are still what a consultation with Joshua looks like today: a real conversation first, a stencil second.

He specializes in black-and-grey realism — portraits, wildlife, large-scale sleeves and back pieces, and cover-up redesigns that need real planning, not just darker ink. Every project starts with mapping how the piece sits on your specific anatomy, how it reads at a distance ten years from now, and how it will actually heal in Las Vegas' climate.
