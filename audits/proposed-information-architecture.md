## Consolidation Principles

- Healing-database variants should merge into one excellent tattoo healing hub unless a page has real Work of Art documentation.
- Location pages should consolidate into `/visit/` or `/visit/near-strip/` unless they contain unique route, parking, travel, hotel, event, or local visitor information.
- Keep legacy URLs until 301 redirect mapping is complete.
